ABOUT US

Practical financial expertise for growing businesses.

CLARITA Associates is a Dhaka-based professional services firm providing specialized VAT, income tax, and accounting solutions to businesses operating in Bangladesh.

We support local enterprises, foreign investors, and non-profit organizations in meeting statutory obligations under the VAT and Supplementary Duty Act 2012, the Income Tax Act 2023, and related NBR regulations.

Our team brings hands-on experience across manufacturing, trading, services, RMG, NGO/NPO, and technology sectors — working as an extension of clients' finance functions.

OUR SERVICES

End-to-end compliance & accounting support

Services tailored to the requirements of NBR and RJSC and the practical needs of management.

VAT

VAT Registration & Return Filing

BIN registration, Mushak-6.3 / 9.1 return preparation and online submission, VDS compliance, Mushak-4.3 declarations, VAT audit support and representation.

TAX

Income Tax

TIN registration, individual and corporate e-filing, advance tax computation, TDS compliance and certificates, assessment representation and appeal support.

GL

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Monthly bookkeeping, general ledger maintenance, bank reconciliation, budgeting, forecasting, IFRS/IAS-based financial statements and management reporting.

AUD

Audit & Assurance Support

Coordination with statutory auditors, audit-ready financial packs, internal control reviews and support for special-purpose audits.

PAY

Payroll Processing

Monthly payroll computation, salary TDS calculation and certificates, provident fund and gratuity record-keeping and statutory payroll reporting.
